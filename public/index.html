Know your number

The PSI scale

PSI is an index, not a concentration.
These are the 24-hour PSI bands.

0–50Good
51–100Moderate
101–200Unhealthy
201–300Very unhealthy
Above 300Hazardous

1-hour PM2.5 bands

PM2.5 is a concentration in µg/m³.
These are NEA’s 1‑hour bands.

0–55Band I · Normal
56–150Band II · Elevated
151–250Band III · High
251 and aboveBand IV · Very high
